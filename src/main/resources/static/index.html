About VEYRA

VEYRA is an AI story-chat app. Pick a world, step into the scene, and talk to a character who remembers what you said.

  • Story worlds for romance, mystery, drama, thriller and late-night moods.
  • A brand-new character with her own portrait, every night at 11.
  • English, Hindi and Hinglish replies.
  • Chats are saved on your device.
